Our Legal Guarantee (100% Genuine Land)

At MANOKAMNA MARKETING, we understand that buying land is one of the most significant lifetime decisions for any family. To completely eliminate any risk, litigation, or fraudulent activity, we enforce a strict 5-Point Verification System before any property layout is offered to the public.

Our core philosophy revolves around transparent transactions, zero-hassle Registry, and absolute physical security. This has made us Patna's most trusted real estate brand since 2007.

1. 30-Years Clear Title Search

Before launching any township, our in-house legal experts perform a rigorous background search up to the past 30 years. We verify:

  • "Khatian" documents, land deeds, mutation certificates, and updated land revenue receipts (LPC).
  • Non-Encumbrance Certificate (NEC) to ensure the land is free from any existing mortgage, bank loan, or legal disputes.
  • Absolute single-owner clarity or registered partition deeds among ancestral family owners.

2. Transparent Registry & Possession Agreement

We ensure that all documentary proceedings are simple, legal, and clearly recorded:

  • Transparent mutual agreements outlining precise roads width (e.g., 30ft, 40ft wide internal pathways), demarcations, and basic township developments.
  • On-time, seamless registration process guided completely by our office staff at the Patna Registration Office.
  • Direct registry upon standard booking agreements, ensuring complete piece of mind for the buyer.

3. Immediate Physical Possession & Demarcation

A major concern for land buyers in Bihar is boundary disputes. We solve this permanently with our immediate physical hand-over:

  • Every plot booked is physically demarcated and separated with secure brick pillars right after the agreement.
  • Buyers are granted physical possession of the plot immediately, allowing them to initiate personal boundary construction.
  • Planned township security keeps your boundaries safe from any outer encroachment.

4. Regulatory Compliance & Master Plan

All our projects (such as near IT Colony Bihta or Smart City Townships) align with future master plans of Patna Metropolitan Authority:

  • No "greenbelt" or restricted agriculture land is sold under residential categories.
  • Layout designs are optimized for future utility lines, sewerage pipelines, and wide road access.
  • Our projects fully respect the Bihar Government RERA standards and master planning guidelines.

5. Zero Hidden Cost Promise

We are dedicated to honest relationships with our clients. Our pricing policy guarantees:

  • Complete transparency on registry charges, stamp duties, and processing expenses.
  • No hidden maintenance or developmental charges are asked after the final booking is signed.
  • Clear payment plans tailored to your budget constraints without any penalties or hidden interests.
